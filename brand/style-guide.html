Brand Style Guide

MĀ Wellness & Yoga · Victoria, BC

01 · Logo

The MĀ mark

The flowing spirograph lines represent the continuous, cyclical nature of practice. The macron over the Ā connects to Sanskrit roots — honouring the tradition that lives in every class.

Primary · Saffron
Reversed · White
Mono · Bark

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "M" on all sides of the logo mark.

Minimum Size

The logo should never appear smaller than 30px wide on screen or 10mm in print to maintain legibility of the spirograph lines.

Background Usage

Use the saffron logo on light backgrounds, the white logo on dark backgrounds, and the bark logo for monochrome print.

Don't

Never stretch, rotate, add drop shadows, change the proportions, or place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without sufficient contrast.

05 · Cards & Containers

Content patterns

Containers use subtle borders and generous padding. Hover states are soft — never jarring. The sindoor accent marks important callouts.

See Each Person

Everyone is worth being seen exactly as they are. Our approach is not to "fix" anyone.

Honour the Individual

The most important thing brought to each training is the individual.

This understanding equips teachers with basic techniques to work with marginalized populations and students recovering from physical or emotional trauma, grief, or loss.

06 · Voice & Tone

How MĀ speaks

Warm, grounded, and inclusive. The voice honours tradition without being inaccessible. Language is inviting, never prescriptive.

Honour the journey

"Find authentic yogic living in your own way."

Prescribe a path

"You must follow these steps to become a real yogi."

See the whole person

"Everyone is worth being seen exactly as they are."

Imply something is broken

"We'll fix your practice and transform you."

Invite participation

"You become not only a student but an active participant."

Create hierarchy

"Our expert teachers will mold you into shape."
